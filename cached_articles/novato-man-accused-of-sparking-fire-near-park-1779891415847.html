Novato man accused of sparking fire near park

By Cameron Macdonald | cmacdonald@marinij.com
PUBLISHED: May 26, 2026 at 12:54 PM PDT | UPDATED: May 26, 2026 at 1:19 PM PDT
Getting your Trinity Audio player ready...

A Novato man was accused of starting a fire near a playground.

Authorities responded around 6:30 a.m. Monday to a report of a man burning dry leaves under a large tree on Novato Boulevard near Miwok Park, Novato police Cpl. Laura Houser said.

Firefighters quickly extinguished the burning leaves and debris before the flames could spread, Houser said. Officers detained the suspect nearby.

Odilio Escobar Lopez, 30, of Novato was arrested on suspicion of unlawfully causing a fire and violating the terms of his probation.

He was convicted last week in a separate case after he pleaded guilty to threatening a person and obstructing four San Rafael police officers in February. He was sentenced to two years of probation.
